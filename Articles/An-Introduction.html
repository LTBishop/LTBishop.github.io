An Introduction

This page serves as a formal introduction to The Library, its purpose, and how to use it.

Welcome

The Occult Studies Library exists primarily as a resource for practitioners. While the Library will always attempt an academic tone with rigorous research, the target audience is not academics. The target audience is practitioners, those who wish to learn, and those who wish to expand their own craft. The caretakers of this library are reacting to an issue faced by most practitioners, an information gap. That is, most sources available to practitioners fall into one of two categories. The sources are either modern, made for practitioners, but often use the same few sources time and again, or the source is a high level academic who is giving a historical account but not from the view of a practitioner. Both of these sources are useful in their own way, but frequently lack something important to practitioners which is a wider, in depth, breadth of primary sources to learn from that are viewed and discussed by actual practitioners themselves.

This library attempts to rectify that. We wish to show you the primary source, give context and history for that source, and then give an in depth discussion of the source from the perspective of a practitioner. We are not content to say something existed, and then assume the practitioner has a master's degree in the regional history of an artifact to understand the context that it exists in. Nor are we to simply rely on the commonly shared and popularized resources that have been the only well of knowledge that practitioners have felt was made for them time and again, from Agrippa to Gardner. We instead seek to delve into history, select fascinating items, provide the historical and cultural context for those items, and then discuss them with you with the respect that we too believe them to discuss real phenomena rather than myth or superstition.

To this end, the library has decided to uphold a list of values. These values are to ensure that we stay on course, and provide the best source we are able to provide. The first is a commitment to anonymity. We do not, nor ever will, collect information from any who visit the library, nor will we open ourselves to ways that information may be collected. The authors who write entries and articles for the library may retain or disclose their identity at their sole discretion. It saddens us to say that there are still threats to those who practice, but we will honor that reality by reducing vulnerability where we are able.

Additionally, the library is and will always be free. This library was created to make knowledge as accessible as possible, and to this end we will never require anything from a visitor. The creators of this library do not profit from it in any way other than the pleasure of contributing. We do not request funds, we do not have a way to donate funds, this library is free as all knowledge should be when possible. Additionally, by not having a way to receive funds we are not exposed to any personal information from visitors. This considerably assists in our first goal of maintaining anonymity for visitors.

Lastly, we commit to being made by fellow occultists and truth seekers. While I respect the academic community I fear that there are many who trivialize occultism to the explanation solely in the material. As such, any who write for this website will be practitioners with some belief in the metaphysical. There is no requirement to any specific tradition, nor amount of belief required, nor other bar to meet other than those who write are indeed practitioners with a belief in the metaphysical.

Thank you for reading. I hope you enjoy the library and all that it may offer.
