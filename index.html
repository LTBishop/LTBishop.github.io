Welcome

The Occult Studies Library is a free resource for practitioners of all paths. This library has been made with an academic intent, as a place for others to grow and learn with spells, artifacts, and grimoires they may never be exposed to otherwise. However, unlike most academic resources, this library is and will be maintained solely by practitioners.

To learn more of our values and mission, please visit the Articles tab and read An Introduction. The library is an independent project maintained by practitioners acting in a private capacity. No curator claims any accreditation relevant to the materials discussed, and nothing published here should be treated as academically certified, legally viable, or professionally authoritative. We are practitioners sharing research and perspective with other practitioners.
