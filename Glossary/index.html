Glossary

Terms and Tag Definitions

Definitions used by this archive for terms and tags.

Term Definition
Charaktêres A charaktêr, or kharaktere, is a symbol with the form of writing but without a known meaning, or any meaning. These symbols would often appear like writing but lack any phonetic or semantic meaning.
Evocation A type of summoning wherein the practitioner calls out to an entity and expects it to appear or inhabit a certain external location as opposed to possessing or inhabiting, at least partially, the practitioner, another person, or an object. See: Invocation
Fascimile A Fascimile is a highly detailed or exact reproduction of an original artwork or manuscript.
Homeopathic Magic Homeopathic Magic is a type of magic wherein a practitioner performs actions towards on object with an expectation that the same actions or outcomes will affect a subject. Often homeopathic magic requires a "taglock" or some way to link the object and the subject on an energetic level.
Invocation A type of summoning wherein the practitioner calls out to an entity and expects it to possess or inhabit, at least partially, the practitioner, another person, or an object, as opposed to appearing or inhabiting a certain external location. See: Evocation
Middle Ages For Occult Studies Library classification, “Middle Ages” refers to works dated 500–1499 CE.
Prosciption Figure A proscription figure is a type of clay doll used in spell craft. Typically, the doll has the name of the subject inscribed on its chest. Actions are then taken against the doll, such as stabbing, spitting on it, or soaking it in urine. The doll is often destroyed at the end of the spell it is used in.
Sympathetic Magic Sympathetic Magic is a type of magic wherein a practitioner performs actions towards on object with an expectation that the same actions or outcomes will affect a subject. Often homeopathic magic requires a "taglock" or some way to link the object and the subject on an energetic level.
Taglock A taglock is something that is energetically linked to a subject. Taglocks often operate by having some likeness to the subject, either being something unique to them, touched or used by them often, or is biological material of the subject. Taglocks include things such as the name of the subject, a picture of them, articles of clothing they wear often, as well as their hair or any bodily excrements of theirs.
Voces Magicae Pronouncable, but non-linguistic strings of sound. Often found in languages or scripts that differ from the main language used.
